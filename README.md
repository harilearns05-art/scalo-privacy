# Scale Puzzle Privacy Policy

App: Scale Puzzle (com.scalo.app). Developer: Scalo Games. Contact: harilearns05@gmail.com. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

## What the app stores on your phone
Progress (stars, streak, badges, collection, friends list names, settings, your display name). This stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall.

## What is sent online (only when you use online features)
Online Race, Quick Match, Friend Groups, Party Race, Tournaments, Ask-a-friend and the Daily Hall of Fame send: your display name, a random player id, room/group codes, race progress, finish times and scores. This is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database (Google Cloud) over HTTPS to run these features. Other players in the same room, group or day can see your display name and times. The app has no login, and it does not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and it has no chat. Google Firebase may also process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the service. Online data is deleted on request.

## Giant community puzzles
The Giant Jigsaw, Giant Maze and Giant Slide Puzzle are shared by all players for about a month. When you play them, the app sends and stores in Google Firebase Realtime Database: your display name, a T-shirt colour you choose, a random player id, which pieces, checkpoints or moves you completed, the time you completed them, and your totals. This information is PUBLIC inside the game: every player can see your display name and T-shirt on the parts you solved and in the top-players list. Do not use your real full name. Offensive names are blocked. Online data is deleted on request (see Your choices).

## Ads
This version of the app has no ads. If ads are added in a future update, this policy will be updated first to explain what data the ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect, such as device identifiers and ad interaction data. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Sensors and permissions
Accelerometer (tilt the ball; the data is not sent anywhere), vibration, and internet (for online races).

## Children
Scale Puzzle is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will delete the related online data.

## Your choices
Delete local data: Settings > Reset all progress, or uninstall the app. To ask for deletion of your online data, email harilearns05@gmail.com with your display name.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
